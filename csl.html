What is ENpower's Composite Skills Lab?

Composite Skills Labs go beyond infrastructure. Schools must align infrastructure, curriculum, teacher capacity, timetables, assessment, and showcases while sustaining joyful learning. Traditional STEM models fall short as CSL focuses on problem-solving, design thinking, entrepreneurship, and real-world application.

✦

ENpower delivers a complete Grades 6–8 model integrating CBSE, Kaushal Bodh, and 110+ hours of experiential learning. It synchronises curriculum, teacher training, assessment, and school calendars into one connected system that builds confident, future-ready learners.
