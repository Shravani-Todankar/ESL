What Is ENpower's Tech Innovation Lab?

ENpower's Tech Innovation Lab is a powerful innovation space that goes far beyond a traditional STEM lab, serving as a launchpad for young innovators.

Our Tech Innovation Architecture
Currently Exploring

The model is built on 6 integrated tech pillars: Smart Systems, AI Studio, Coding, Robotics, Designing, and Emerging Technologies, giving students hands-on exposure to 'Tech of the Future' — electronics, AI, programming, automation, product design, and future-ready domains like AR/VR, green tech, and cybersecurity.

Learning is delivered through a framework powered by Design Thinking, Enquiry-based learning, and continuous Teacher Training & Handholding.
